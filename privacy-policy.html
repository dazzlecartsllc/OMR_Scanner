Privacy Policy

OMR Sheet Scanner & Checker (Android package com.minigameisland.omrscanner)
Published by Mini Island Studio · Effective date: 31 October 2026

In short: the app reads and grades OMR sheets on your phone. Your sheet pictures, answer keys, roll numbers and results stay on your device and are never uploaded to us. We do not ask you to create an account.

1. Who we are

Mini Island Studio ("we", "us") publishes the app "OMR Sheet Scanner & Checker". You can contact us at dazzlecartsllc@gmail.com.

2. What the app does with your data

The app reads photos or PDF pages of OMR (bubble) answer sheets and grades them against an answer key that you enter. All of this processing happens on your device.

  • Pictures you scan, answer keys, roll or registration numbers, exam names and results are stored only in the app's storage on your device.
  • We do not receive, copy, sell or have access to this data.
  • Reports you export (Word, Excel/CSV or PDF) are saved or shared only where you choose, for example in your files or through another app you pick.

3. Camera, photos and files

  • Camera: when you tap to take a photo, the app opens your phone's own camera app and receives the picture you take. The app does not request the camera permission and does not record anything in the background.
  • Photos and PDFs: the app opens only the picture or PDF that you select.

4. Accounts and personal information

The app has no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts or any other personal information.

5. Advertising

The app includes the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) software development kit so that advertising can be offered in the app now or in a future version. When ads are shown, or when the SDK is active, Google may collect and use the following, according to its own policies (policies.google.com/privacy):

You can reset your advertising ID, delete it, or opt out of ads personalisation at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Google > Ads).

Your sheet pictures, answer keys and results are never given to the ad provider.

6. Children

The app is intended for teachers, examiners, students and exam organisers aged 18 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Data retention and deletion

Data created in the app stays on your device until you remove it. You can delete exams and results inside the app, or remove everything by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no copy of your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

8. Security

Your sheets and results are not sent to us, so they remain under your control on your device. Ad requests, when they occur, are sent to the ad provider over secure connections.

9. Third-party sites and apps

If you share a report through another app (such as email or a messaging app), that app's own privacy policy applies to what you share.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date at the top shows the latest version. If a change is significant, we will say so in the app's release notes.
